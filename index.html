Sorting tips
  • Rank by priority first. Use Manual order and move the pieces you want most to the top of each category, from the menu on each card.
  • Pin what you will buy this week. Pinned items stay on top in every sort.
  • Sort by price, low to high, to keep to a budget. Flip it to high to low to see which pieces take the biggest share.
  • Sort by shop before checkout. Several items from one shop can reach free delivery together.
  • Use oldest first to clear the backlog. Anything that has sat in your cart for weeks is a buy or drop decision.
  • Keep three to five per category. If two items do the same job, remove the weaker one.
